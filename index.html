01 / CAD & Mechanical Design

X3 Roller Coaster

A SolidWorks mechanical assembly exploring independent seat rotation and track-guided motion.

SolidWorks 3D Modeling Assembly Design Motion Constraints

Project Overview

For our Computer-Aided Design final project, our team designed a small-scale roller coaster inspired by the X2 ride at Six Flags.

The assembly was designed to demonstrate two types of movement: independent seat rotation under gravity and movement of the seat assembly along a track.

Engineering & Design Process

The project involved modeling individual components, integrating them into a larger assembly, and applying motion constraints to simulate the intended movements.

Design challenges included developing the track geometry, aligning components from different team members, and troubleshooting assembly constraints.

Results & Takeaways

The completed CAD assembly demonstrated the intended sliding and rotating motions in simulation.

The project provided experience with assembly constraints, mechanical tolerances, component integration, and collaborative CAD development.
